Slide Refinement Goal Evaluation 🔍

For each paper, judge whether the three refined slide decks satisfy the goals they were refined against.

Instructions

  1. Five random papers are chosen for evaluation.
  2. Each paper has three refined slide decks (System A, B, C). generated by three different methods and the ordering is randomized.
  3. The slide decks are refined by each method such that it satisfies the goals.
  4. For each of the 5 goals, mark True if the refined deck satisfies it, False if not.
  5. Goals differem> across decks even for the same paper — that's expected.
  6. When every goal is answered, click Submit.
  7. Important: Evaluate only based on whether the goals are satisfied by the slide deck, not by the content or visual quality of the slide.

Goal Categories

1. Content Inclusion/Exclusion   2. Narrative Structure   3. Figure & Table Usage   4. Style & Wording   5. Deck Composition
